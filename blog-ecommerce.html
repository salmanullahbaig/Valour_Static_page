E-commerce Infrastructure Built to Survive Peak Traffic

Your store's busiest hour of the year is worth more than the quietest month. E-commerce engineering is the art of building for that hour without bankrupting the rest.

Speed is revenue

The data has been consistent for a decade: every hundred milliseconds of added page load measurably cuts conversion. We treat performance as a product feature with a budget — image pipelines, caching layers, edge delivery and lean frontends are designed in from the start, not retrofitted after the first slow Black Friday.

The same discipline applies to checkout. Every extra field, redirect or spinner in the payment flow is a place where a customer with a full cart changes their mind.

Nobody remembers a fast checkout. Everybody remembers a crashed one.

Order infrastructure that scales on demand

Behind the storefront, we build the unglamorous machinery that makes commerce work: inventory that stays consistent under concurrent orders, payment integration with proper idempotency and reconciliation, and order pipelines that survive traffic spikes through queueing and autoscaling rather than hope.

Campaign days are rehearsed, not gambled on — we load-test against multiples of peak traffic and know the breaking points before your marketing team finds them.

What we typically deliver in e-commerce

  • High-conversion storefronts and product catalogs
  • Payment gateway integration and reconciliation
  • Inventory and order management systems
  • Sales analytics and customer behavior dashboards
  • Load testing and peak-season readiness reviews

Know what sells, and why

A store generates data with every click, and most of it goes unused. We wire analytics through the full funnel — acquisition, browsing, cart, checkout, repeat purchase — so merchandising decisions are made on evidence instead of instinct.

Whether you are launching a storefront or reinforcing one that creaks under load, we can help you build for the hour that matters.
